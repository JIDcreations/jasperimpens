1447

“A walk through Antwerp's diamond district.”

Type
Identity and app concept
Place
Antwerp
Role
Design

A visual system and app concept for a walking route. A timeline of stops, a live map, and the distance to the next shop along the way.

Challenge

Antwerp's diamond district has centuries of history, but it is hard to read while walking through it. 1447 turns the streets into a route you can follow, with the history attached to real places.

Approach

A timeline that starts at 1447 and numbers the stops. A dark map with the route in yellow. Every stop opens as a card with a photo, its name and how far away it is, and a single Start button to begin walking. The visual system pairs crisp, stone-like surfaces with a warm gold.

Result

A full visual identity and app concept: a timeline, a route and a set of stop cards that hang together as one product.
