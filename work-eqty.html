EQTY

“Beleggen als proces.”

Type
Mobile app
Sector
Education
Context
Final-year project
Role
Design and code

A Dutch-language app that teaches investing as a process. An onboarding quiz, themed lessons, and reflection questions that personalise what comes next.

Challenge

Investing is usually taught as a list of products. EQTY starts from a different idea, printed on its first lesson: investing is a structured decision process, not a loose action. The app had to make that process learnable without feeling like homework.

Approach

An onboarding quiz asks what already sounds familiar (shares, ETFs, spreading risk, long-term investing) and places the learner. Lessons are grouped in themes with a clear progress bar and one obvious next step. After each lesson a reflection question asks what you learned, and your answer shapes the lessons that follow. Sources, videos and a glossary sit within reach.

Result

A complete, working app flow, delivered with a full demo, a process document, a poster and a launch reel.
