Kind of work

  • Specter

    “Cut like architecture.”

    A brutalist webshop for a made-to-measure tailor in Antwerp. Nine pieces, nothing off the rack, everything by appointment. The site had to carry exactly that restriction.

    • Webshop
    • Tailoring
    • WordPress

  • Studio Klei

    “Keramiek voor elke dag.”

    A warm, Dutch-language webshop for a ceramics studio in Ghent. Categories, workshops and a cart, built to feel as personal as the handwork it sells.

    • Webshop
    • Ceramics
    • WordPress

  • Keikoku Atelier

    “Restraint is the material we use most.”

    A calm, image-led site for an architecture bureau designing houses meant to last a hundred years. Four commissions on three continents, told in as much image as text.

    • Website
    • Architecture
    • Netlify

  • Elevator Antwerp

    “Telling stories with contemporary art.”

    The website for a gallery without a fixed address. Elevator Antwerp takes over temporary spaces in the city, curates contemporary art there, and moves on. The works stay centre stage.

    • Website
    • Art and gallery

  • EQTY

    “Beleggen als proces.”

    A Dutch-language app that teaches investing as a process. An onboarding quiz, themed lessons, and reflection questions that personalise what comes next.

    • Mobile app
    • Education
    • Design and code

  • 1447

    “A walk through Antwerp's diamond district.”

    A visual system and app concept for a walking route. A timeline of stops, a live map, and the distance to the next shop along the way.

    • Identity
    • Mobile app
    • Concept
