Passion

I design and build for a living. Everything else is mostly a bike, a camera and a language I'm still learning.

On the bike

Most weekends I ride with Club9000. Early starts, a group in single file, and a few hours where nothing else needs deciding. Cycling is the closest thing I have to a second job, equal parts obsession and recovery from the first one.

It also keeps showing up in my work. My sense of pace, patience and detail comes from long rides more than from any tool.
